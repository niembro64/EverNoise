EverNoise for Android

Your sound. All night.

A native, free Android app. Continuous on-device sound, six mix choices per band, saved App Loudness, background audio, Lock Screen controls, a Home Screen widget and a Quick Settings tile.

Simple installation

  1. Download the signed APK above, or add the repository in F-Droid.
  2. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser or F-Droid. Follow the system prompt for that app.
  3. Open EverNoise. Your sound begins automatically; use the notification, Lock Screen or app to pause.

Some phones apply extra battery restrictions. The Settings screen includes a link to Android’s app battery settings. Calls, audio-focus changes, force-stop and shutdown can interrupt playback.
