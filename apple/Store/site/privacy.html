EverNoise

Your sound. Your privacy.

EverNoise does not collect, store, or share personal data.

The app

All audio is generated locally on your device. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. It does not access your microphone, location, photos, contacts, or health information. It works offline and does not send network requests during playback.

Support and external links

If you choose to email support, your email address and the contents of your message are used only to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive personal information in support messages. You may request deletion of your support correspondence by contacting the same address.

Opening a support or privacy link launches your browser. This website uses no analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Its hosting and delivery providers may process standard technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. Your email provider and Apple’s services operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated to describe them. The current app collects no personal data.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Eric Niemeyer.

Effective November 14, 2026. Also applies to earlier versions named Just Brown Noise, Persistent Noise, or Endless Noise.
