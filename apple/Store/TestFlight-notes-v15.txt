Build 15: calmer palette and smoother ribbons

• Stereo starts on when no preference has been saved. A previously saved Off remains Off.
• Enabled switches are visibly soft green; disabled switches are gray.
• EverNoise uses a lighter rounded title font.
• Muted rose, sage, and periwinkle replace pure RGB. The infinity and background share a balanced six-hue family.
• Four enormous ribbons now move within one fixed rendering surface, with no animated SwiftUI texture transforms. Flow, drift, and rotation are slower.

Please watch the largest ribbons for any remaining jitter on your iPhone, and check switching, pause/resume, and saved settings. Test with your usual headphones or speakers. The audio filters, gains, and stereo DSP are unchanged from build 14. The known 44.1 kHz conversion-level difference from earlier builds remains documented in the audio audit.

Shader continuity and GPU cost were measured on the Mac; physical iPhone cadence, overnight continuity, and battery consumption are not yet measured for this build.
