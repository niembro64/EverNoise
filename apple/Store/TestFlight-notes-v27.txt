EverNoise 1.1 (27)

Max now shows a wider, more detailed output spectrogram. The logarithmic axis spans 2 Hz to 48 kHz; a dashed line marks the 24 kHz measurement limit. The shaded area above it is outside the generated signal's measurable range.

Frequency determines color: muted red for lows, green for mids and blue for highs, blended using the equal-gain crossover curves. Brighter means more measured power. Colors stay fixed when you change the mix, and the brightness scale stays fixed too.

Deep bass gets a longer analysis window with about 0.18 Hz bin spacing. Allow roughly six seconds for the first measurement; the lowest frequencies respond more gradually. Playback starts immediately as before.

Try Max in Settings, change the Low/Mid/High balance, and check that the matching frequencies brighten or dim. Check pause, resume, screen lock and switching apps. Off disables visual processing; Med remains the default.

The sound engine, saved mix, loudness adjustment and peak protection are unchanged. Analysis observes the final generated audio before system volume and speaker processing.
