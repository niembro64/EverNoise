1.0.1 (23). Apple Watch is excluded at the user's request.

## Implementation

- Native tvOS 17+ application under the existing bundle ID and App Store record.
- The production DSP, playback state machine, audio driver, audio service, and
  Metal shader are byte-identical to `power-complementary-v1-build-23`.
- Landscape controls sized for television, a visible remote-focus outline,
  shared five-choice band buttons, Mono/Stereo and animation settings, and both
  onscreen and Siri Remote Play/Pause control.
- Shared infinity/ribbon renderer; hidden views suspend animation without
  touching audio. A Sendable size value replaces a captured GeometryProxy,
  preserving the same geometry and avoiding a Swift concurrency warning.
- Layered infinity icons and Top Shelf artwork generated by the checked-in
  Swift script. The screenshot shows the Release app at its 3/3/2 default.
- TV description, promotional text, support information, screenshot, and
  review notes are saved. Apple's required TV privacy-policy text explains
  offline generation, locally saved preferences, no personal-data collection,
  support correspondence, and the separate privacy practices of Apple services.

## Validation

- All **40 core tests** passed after narrowing the platform scope.
- All **27 iOS integration and 5 iOS UI tests** passed during the port.
- All **8 tvOS audio integration tests** passed, including every mix, stereo
  changes, pause fades, rapid pause/resume, and background-session configuration.
- Both **tvOS remote UI tests** passed: every band remains reachable, Settings
  preserves playback, a single Play/Pause event toggles once, and returning
  from the Home screen advances the audio clock without restarting its engine.
- Final Release simulator screen visually checked at 1920x1080. Text, focus,
  infinity, selected levels, and pause control are visible with no clipping.
- Exported IPA passes strict signature verification. Its App Store profile is
  for tvOS, disables debugging, and enables beta reports. Background audio,
  compiled Metal, privacy manifest, app icon, and Top Shelf keys are present.
- Xcode distribution signing succeeded during export; no development device
  registration was needed for the App Store/TestFlight package.
