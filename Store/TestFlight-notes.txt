Build 23: a fundamentally new sound engine

• Low, Mid, and High now use complementary bands with a warm pink-weighted base.
• Equal settings preserve the same continuous base spectrum through the overlaps.
• The same saved choices intentionally sound different from build 22.
• Default buttons remain 3 / 3 / 2, counting Off as the first button.
• Digital output level is lower; use your device volume for a comfortable comparison.
• Stereo, smooth level changes, and background playback remain.
• The old sound is preserved in the public Git tag sound-baseline-build-22.

Please compare the new tone on your usual speaker/headphones, try all band levels and Mono/Stereo, and test locked-screen/background playback for longer than eight hours. Report any distortion, clicking, unexpected silence, or interruptions. The 30-hour checks are accelerated software tests; listening and overnight device results are still needed.
