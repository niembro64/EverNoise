Build 19: three animation levels

• Low: a still infinity, no background ribbons.
• Med: an animated infinity, no background ribbons. This is the default.
• High: the animated infinity and floating background ribbons.
• The selected level is remembered. A saved old animations-off choice becomes Low.
• Switching levels leaves your sound playing continuously.

Please try all three levels, reopen the app to check persistence, and confirm Low remains still. Audio generation and playback behavior are unchanged.
