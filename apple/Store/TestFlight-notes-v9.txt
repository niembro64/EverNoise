Build 9 is now named EverNoise and uses the RGB infinity app icon. The name is updated on the home screen, in the app, and in lock-screen playback controls.

Low, Mid, and High each have five choices: Off, 1, 2, 3, and 4. The active levels use equal 6 dB steps (-18, -12, -6, and 0 dB). Existing settings are preserved. Low 4 / Mid Off / High Off is the original Brown sound; all three Off produces silence.

The background stays black. Red, green, and blue picker buttons and a small infinity mark show the color of your mix. Changes fade smoothly without restarting playback.

The audio generator and playback logic are unchanged from builds 7 and 8. The pause button and system volume controls remain. Playback starts when you open the app and continues in the background or with the screen locked when iOS permits.

Please verify the infinity icon on your home screen and try playback on your iPhone speaker, including locked-screen and overnight use. Report any harshness, distortion, clicks, gaps, or unexpected stops.

Free, offline, and no playback time limit.
